Reproducibility & Independent Reproduction

Oracle Condition:
  • Flaky Fault (One-off): First execution fails (HTTP 500 transient blip). Subsequent reproduction in new context succeeds (HTTP 200). Result must remain unconfirmed.
  • Stable Fault: Every attempt consistently fails (HTTP 500). Result is upgraded to confirmed.
