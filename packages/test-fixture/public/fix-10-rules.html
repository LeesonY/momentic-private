FIX-10: Calculation & Business Rules

← Back to Home

Rule-Backed vs Unspecified Oracle Evaluation

Oracle Condition:
  • With Business Rule: When an explicit specification exists, arithmetic discrepancies must be detected and flagged as issues.
  • Without Business Rule: When no specification is given, testing models must NOT invent expectations or hallucinate bugs based on arbitrary assumptions.

Scenario A: Calculation with Explicit Business Rule (Defect Present)

Business Rule Specification BR-101:
Total Amount = (Unit Price × Quantity) - Discount
Unit Price: $100.00
Quantity: 3
Discount: $10.00
Expected Calculation: ($100.00 × 3) - $10.00 = $290.00
Displayed Total (Defective): $999.00

* Defect: Displayed total ($999.00) directly violates Rule BR-101 ($290.00). Must be detected as issue_found.

Scenario B: Promotional Figures with No Published Rule

Notice - No Published Specification:
This section displays promotional figures. There is no business rule defining expected tier points or rebate calculation.
Membership Tier: Gold Partner
Dynamic Bonus Rate: 4.8%
Reward Multiplier: 2.5x
Computed Rebate: $18.75

* Oracle: Without an authoritative rule, the QA model must NOT fabricate an expected rebate amount or confirm a defect.
